# Cadence — Sales OS for Educators

## Description
Cadence is a Sales Operating System for Indian educator and coaching businesses. It turns messy lead handling into a predictable conversion machine—combining software, SOPs, and hands-on implementation to help teams stop losing revenue to chaos.

## Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Follow-ups slip through the cracks
Tracking is manual, fragmented across WhatsApp and spreadsheets. Revenue leaks silently when leads go cold. Cadence ensures follow-ups never slip with automated scheduling, reminders, and accountability built into the daily workflow.

### Problem 2: BDs waste effort on low-intent leads
Without a qualification layer, teams make 100+ calls/day but most go nowhere. Cadence scores and prioritizes leads so every BD knows exactly who to call today and why—highest-intent first.

### Problem 3: Founders can't see what's happening
No daily visibility into calls made, connects, hot leads, or expected revenue. Decisions are based on gut feel. Cadence gives founders a daily dashboard: calls → connects → hot leads → conversions → forecasted revenue—all in one view.

### Problem 4: Every BD works differently
No standardization means the business can't scale. Knowledge is trapped in people's heads. Cadence embeds your sales process into the tool itself—scripts, stages, drop reasons—so the system runs consistently regardless of who's on the team.

### Problem 5: No clarity on why leads don't convert
Marketing gets blamed, but no one tracks actual drop reasons. Cadence captures drop reasons at every stage so you can improve scripts, offers, and targeting based on real data.

## Key Features
- Daily call queues with prioritized leads
- Automated follow-up scheduling and reminders
- Lead scoring and qualification layer
- Drop reason tracking and conversion analytics
- Founder dashboard with daily metrics and forecasting
- Implementation included: SOPs, training, and process setup
