# Product Roadmap

## Sections

### 1. Product Catalog
The central hub for defining and managing all products — including product types, styles, specifications, attributes, images, and documents — across any vertical.

### 2. Bill of Materials
Hierarchical breakdown of materials, components, and packaging for each product with cost rollups, version history, and approval workflows.

### 3. Sourcing & Suppliers
Manage supplier and factory relationships, send and track RFQs, compare quotes, and coordinate material procurement across the supply chain.

### 4. Calendar & Milestones
Timeline management for product development stages, critical path tracking, deadline enforcement, and cross-functional milestone approvals.

### 5. Dashboard & Reporting
Executive-level KPIs, product health metrics, pipeline visibility, and configurable reports for tracking progress across the entire product lifecycle.
